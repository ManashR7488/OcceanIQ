 OcceanIQ: An AI-Powered Marine Data & Analytics Platform




OcceanIQ is a comprehensive, cloud-native marine data analytics platform developed for the Centre for Marine Living Resources and Ecology (CMLRE), under the Ministry of Earth Sciences. The platform addresses the critical need for a centralized system to aggregate, analyze, and visualize vast and varied oceanographic datasets.

Core capabilities include:

Unified Data Hub: Integrates over 50 global and regional data sources, including OBIS, WoRMS, and iNaturalist, providing a single point of access to over 10,000 catalogued species and environmental layers.
Advanced Analytics Modules: Features dedicated modules for Otolith Analysis (2D/3D shape analysis), eDNA Processing, and Taxonomic Management.
Interactive Mapping: A multi-layered GIS interface for visualizing species occurrences, environmental data (sea temperature, currents), and ecosystem health metrics.
AI-Powered Co-Pilot: An integrated AI assistant featuring:
Predictive Analytics: Forecasts oceanographic trends and identifies anomalies.
Correlation Engine: Uncovers relationships between environmental factors and species distribution.
Dual-Mode Chatbot: A conversational AI for quick queries and a "Research Mode" that leverages an n8n agent to generate comprehensive, downloadable PDF research reports on any marine topic.



The study of marine ecosystems is often hindered by fragmented and disparate data sources, impeding comprehensive analysis and timely decision-making. OcceanIQ is a comprehensive, AI-powered marine data analytics platform designed to address this challenge by unifying diverse oceanographic data into a single, interactive hub for researchers and policymakers. The platform integrates over 50 global and regional datasets, providing a multi-layered GIS interface for advanced spatial analysis. A central feature is its AI co-pilot, built on Google's Gemini model, which offers predictive trend analysis, correlation discovery, and a novel "Research Mode" that leverages an n8n automation workflow to generate comprehensive, downloadable PDF research documents on demand. By providing a seamless workflow from data aggregation to AI-driven insight generation, OcceanIQ significantly accelerates the research lifecycle and empowers data-informed marine conservation efforts.
